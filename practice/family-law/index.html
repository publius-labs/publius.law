Family Law

Clarity in the most sensitive matters.

Publius gives family law attorneys command of the financial, communications, and custody record — so client meetings, hearings, and negotiations are grounded in evidence, not memory.

Capabilities

Financial discovery

Indexes financial documents — bank statements, tax returns, asset disclosures — and surfaces inconsistencies: unreported accounts, timing gaps, contradictions between declarations and supporting records.

Communications review

Searches across emails, texts, and messaging history to find relevant exchanges by topic, date, or participant — without manual scrolling through years of records.

Custody timelines

Assembles chronologies of parenting events, custody exchanges, and incidents from the underlying documents — ready for mediation, hearing prep, or client review.

Outcomes

What this means in practice.

  • Faster preparation for settlement conferences
  • Better-supported asset and custody positions
  • More attorney time on counsel, less on cleanup

Common Questions

Frequently asked

Can Publius help with financial discovery in divorce cases?

Yes. Publius indexes bank statements, tax returns, and asset disclosures in one workspace, flagging inconsistencies — unreported accounts, timing gaps, and contradictions between filings — so attorneys walk into hearings with a complete financial picture.

How does Publius handle sensitive family records?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Each matter has its own isolated workspace. Publius never uses client data to train models, and attorneys retain full control of their files.

Can I use Publius for custody disputes?

Publius builds structured timelines from parenting logs, communications, and incident records — giving attorneys a clear, source-linked chronology for mediation or hearing preparation.
