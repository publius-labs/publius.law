Publius is case intelligence for document-heavy litigation, sold to solo attorneys and small litigation teams. Design for people who read closely and distrust hype: the page should look like a well-run law office, quiet, ordered and sourced.

## Content fundamentals

- Write plain, declarative sentences about the matter, the record and the source. Prefer "matter", "record", "source" and "pilot" to "solution", "platform" and "demo".
- Speak to the attorney as "you" and about the product as "Publius". Do not use "we" for claims about results.
- Make no numeric outcome claims, no superlatives ("best", "revolutionary") and no "enterprise-grade". Describe what the product does, then stop.
- Sentence case for headings and buttons. Eyebrows are uppercase via the `eyebrow` style, never typed in capitals.
- Primary action is "Request a Pilot", with a trailing arrow glyph (→) in buttons and cards. Do not mix in "Request a Demo".
- Three value phrases recur and keep this order: Find the Facts, See the Connections, Prepare with Confidence.
- Real copy to match: "Know the whole case." · "Bring one matter. See what Publius finds." · "Case intelligence for lean litigation teams."
- No emoji.

## Visual foundations

- **Color.** The palette is monochrome and warm. Content sections sit on `background` (ivory) with text in `foreground` (ink). Secondary text is `muted-foreground`; dividers are `hairline` or `border`. There is no brand accent hue: do not add one. Use `destructive` only for errors, always with a word.
- **Dark sepia sections.** The homepage alternates ivory sections with dark ones. The hero is a full-bleed photograph under a sepia filter and a 50–65% near-black overlay, with text in `footer-ink` and the eyebrow at 75% opacity. The "Why Publius", "The Platform", closing CTA and footer sections use the sepia gradient from `footer-sepia-top` through `footer-sepia-mid` at 45% to `footer-sepia-bottom`, with text in `footer-ink`, eyebrows at 50% and supporting copy at 65–70%. The testimonial band is the one flat tint, `#F2EFE8`. Keep this rhythm: light, dark, light, dark.
- **Type.** Headlines in `display-xl` and `heading-card` (Cormorant Garamond, tight tracking, regular weight). Everything else in Inter via `body`, `body-sm`, `nav`, `eyebrow` and `caption`. Never set body copy in the serif.
- **Layout.** Center content in a 1240px container with `gutter` side padding. Sections take `section-y`, then `section-y-lg` from 768px. Legal and long reads narrow to 768px. Left-aligned text, generous whitespace.
- **Shape and depth.** Corners use `radius-base` (2px). Separate areas with 1px `hairline` rules, not shadows or heavy fills. The only gradients are the hero overlay and the sepia sections. The one shadow on the site belongs to the white product-window mockup on a sepia ground.
- **Header.** Sticky, 64px tall, `ivory` at 90% opacity with a light backdrop blur and a `hairline` bottom rule. Logo left, nav links in `nav` and `muted-foreground`, hover to `foreground`.
- **Focus.** Use a solid `ring` outline on every interactive element.
- **Imagery.** The homepage hero is a low-key photograph of an attorney reviewing a binder under a brass desk lamp, run through a sepia filter. Other pages use similar photographs (about, contact, four process phases). Any replacement should be dark, warm and low-saturation so it still sits under the sepia treatment. The product view on the homepage is drawn interface, not a photograph: keep it as drawn interface.

## Iconography

Icons come from Lucide, outlined at 2px stroke (the mobile menu uses `menu`). Arrows inside copy and buttons are the text glyph →. Do not add filled or colored icons.

## Assets

The Publius wordmark is in the Logos group. Use it as supplied, never recolored or redrawn; on the sepia footer apply a CSS invert.
